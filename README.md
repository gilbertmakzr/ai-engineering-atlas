# AI Engineering Insight Atlas

As busy professionals and developers, there are more AI engineering talks than most have time to watch. Sometimes a promising title may turn out into an uninspiring video; or a video description may not give enough context to tell if the video is worth the time investment.

Inspired by the [Lenny's Newsletter Infographic Gallery](https://lennygallery.manus.space/), the AI Engineering Insight Atlas is a quicker way to decide. It gathers practical talks from AI engineering conferences and organisations, then groups them into useful themes and gives each one a short, plain-language summary. The aim is not to replace the original talk. It is to help you find the ones that are likely to be worth your time.

Each summary also features the original video so that you can hear, feel and relate to the speaker. The human connection is something I hope we humans strive to preserve.

## What the application provides

The Atlas currently covers nine themes: System Design, Data & Eval, Reliability, Observability, Safety & Control, Deployment, Knowledge, Developer Workflows and Models & Training.

- A searchable, filterable catalog of AI engineering talks by theme, tag and year
- Short summaries that explain the main idea, why it matters, when to use it and the important caveats
- A responsive detail view with direct links back to the original YouTube talk
- Lightweight tags for cross-cutting topics such as retrieval, agentic coding and model training
